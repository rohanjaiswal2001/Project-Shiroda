02The coast

Where the Konkan meets the sea

  • Shiroda, Sindhudurg: the southern tip of Maharashtra’s Konkan coast, on the Goa border
  • 6 km from Shiroda beach, with Velagar, Redi and Aravali close by
  • Shore Days: a chauffeured jeep, shaded loungers and a beach butler for residents
  • Jet skis, parasailing and a private jetty for sea days
  • On the horizon: a Taj luxury resort by IHCL at Shiroda–Velagar
03The pool

Our own shore

  • A lagoon-style pool with a soft sand beach entry, behind the sports club
  • Arched swim-up bar, cabanas and daybeds
  • A kids’ splash pool and pool volleyball
04Arrival

Getting here

  • Mopa International Airport (GOX): about 25 km, 30 minutes
  • Sindhudurg (Chipi) Airport: about 45 km, an hour
  • By road via NH-66 and SH-123; North Goa within easy reach
  • Mumbai about an hour by air from Mopa
05The front nine

The course

  • 18-hole championship course, built to professional grade
  • Designed to qualify for PGA affiliation
  • Around 140 acres of fairways and greens
  • Walking and cycling paths along the course edge
  • Course designer to be announced

The wellness sanctuary

08The back nine

Toward the hills

  • Two lakes, a peninsula green and championship bunkering
  • The 18th finishes at the sports club
  • 254 acres in all: the course, villas, two clubs, sanctuary and sport

  • Every home includes both clubs, the pool, the sanctuary and Shore Days
